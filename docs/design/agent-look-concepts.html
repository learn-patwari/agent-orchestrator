Agent look: three concepts

Same data in each (roles, states, hand-offs). Pick the feeling you want; the app would drive them from real events.

A. Every role is its own little robot character with a personality. Cards are easy to read on a phone, work with keyboard and screen readers (they are real elements), and the hand-off is a flying envelope between cards. Best if you want friendly and approachable.

B. The Orchestrator sits at the centre like mission control; the team orbits it. Glowing beams show who is talking to whom, packets travel along them on a real hand-off, and rings pulse while an agent works. Best if you want it to feel like a live system and show the "only voice to the human" idea.

C. A delivery line that mirrors your real pipeline: a task crate rides the belt from architect to dev, test, review, QC and production, stopping at each station while it is worked on and changing colour as it passes. Best if you care most about seeing where each piece of work is right now.
